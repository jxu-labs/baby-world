    What might she need?

    Cues overlap. The same fussing can mean tired, hungry or just done with a game. Try the most likely thing first, then the next. This is a guide, not a diagnosis.

    If she seems unwell, has a fever, or crying won't settle, call a parent. In an emergency, call 911 first.
